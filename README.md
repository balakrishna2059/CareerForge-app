# CareerForge - Career Operating System

CareerForge is a comprehensive, all-in-one career management platform designed to help professionals track their job hunt, build their portfolios, and find new opportunities seamlessly.

## ⚠️ Problem Statement
Job seekers constantly juggle multiple disparate platforms to track applications, format resumes, prepare for interviews, and manage their portfolio documents. This fragmentation leads to a disorganized, stressful, and inefficient job search experience.

## 💡 Solution
CareerForge serves as a centralized "Career OS" (Operating System). It unifies job tracking, live job searching, portfolio management, and resume building into a single, cohesive dashboard. 

## 🚀 Novelty
Unlike standard job boards or simple spreadsheets, CareerForge offers:
- **Integrated Drive Ecosystem:** Native Google Drive integration allows users to directly attach project files, internship records, and certificates to their portfolio from their cloud storage.
- **Curated Live Job Board:** Built-in live search featuring instant quick-filters for top tech giants (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, etc.) with real, actionable application links.
- **End-to-End Lifecycle Management:** Handles the complete candidate journey—from tracking learning roadmaps and mock interviews to finalizing applications.

## 🛠️ How It Works & Use Cases
- **Live Job Search:** Browse and filter real-time job openings. Instantly find roles at targeted companies.
- **Portfolio & Experience:** Document your work history, projects, and certifications. Upload supporting documents via local files or Google Drive.
- **Application Tracking:** Kanban-style or list tracking of where you've applied, interview dates, and offer statuses.
- **Preparation:** Utilize built-in tools for resume analysis, cover letter generation, and interview preparation.

## 💻 Technologies Used
- **Frontend:** React 18, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide React (Icons)
- **Backend & Database:** Express.js, Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Cloud Storage)
- **Integrations:** Google Workspace API (Google Drive Picker / Google Identity Services) for file management, third-party Job APIs (JSearch via RapidAPI, Arbeitnow)

## 🔮 Future Scope
- **AI-Powered Insights:** Integration with AI models to automatically analyze resumes and tailor cover letters for specific job descriptions.
- **Calendar Synchronization:** Direct integration with Google Calendar to automatically schedule and track upcoming interview dates.
- **Networking & Mentorship:** A community feature allowing candidates to connect with mentors, peers, and share interview experiences.
- **Advanced Analytics:** Visual dashboards showing application success rates, time-to-hire metrics, and skill-gap analysis to optimize the job hunt.
